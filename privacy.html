Privacy Policy

Aldmere: Kingdom Defense · Last updated October 21, 2026

The short version

Aldmere does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your game progress

Stars, gems, helpers and heroes are saved on your device. On iPhone and iPad, if iCloud is turned on, they are also saved to your own iCloud account so they follow you to other devices. On Android they stay on your device. We cannot see this data.

Game Center

On Apple devices, if you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles that sign-in under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple ID or contact details.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details; we only learn which item was bought so we can deliver it.

Children

Because we collect no personal data, we collect none from children.
